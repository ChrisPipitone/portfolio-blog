Color Theme Explorations

Typography locked: Libre Baskerville + DM Sans. Layout locked: navy nav → grain hero → section bands. These explore color only. Compare against design-preview.html (baseline).

Theme 1 — Gunmetal & Mahogany

Current palette, de-blued. Navy stays for light mode structure. Dark mode shifts to charcoal/gunmetal — no more cold blue everywhere. Mahogany red (#7A2020) replaces the current dark red. Warm gray text.

Theme 2 — Walnut & Olive

Dark walnut replaces navy for structure. Olive green as the accent color. Mahogany red for links and interaction. Warmest, most "leather goods" of the four. Light mode feels like aged parchment. Dark mode like a walnut workshop at night.

Theme 4 — Forge & Grain

Most distinctive. Near-black charcoal structure. Warm brass/amber (#8B6B3A) as the identity accent — name, sub-title, decorative line. Mahogany for links. Sage green tags. Light mode: warm cream with near-black structure. Dark: near-black with amber warmth. Feels like a machined part or a well-oiled tool.
